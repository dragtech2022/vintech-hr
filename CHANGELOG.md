# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-04

First standalone community release. New Python/SQLite application with admin authentication, employee records, weekly shift settings, up to three allowed shifts, CSV export, activity history and Docker/Ubuntu installation.

The earlier `vintech_hr` 0.1.0 ZIP was a Frappe add-on. This standalone source does not share its runtime or database and cannot be installed as its in-place upgrade.
